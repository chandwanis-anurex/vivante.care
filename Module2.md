## Business Rules  
* All workforce requests originate from the dashboard or quick actions.  
* Users only see data permitted by their role.  
* Every KPI supports drill-down into the underlying records.  
* AI recommendations are advisory; final decisions remain with authorized users.  
* Sensitive clinician information remains protected until the appropriate workflow stage.  
* Every action taken from the dashboard is captured in the audit log.  
## My suggestion for Module 3  
The **Create Workforce Request** flow is where most staffing platforms lose users because they rely on long forms.  
I think we can redesign it as an **AI-assisted hiring wizard** that reduces completion time to under **two minutes** while still collecting everything needed for recruiters, compliance, and matching. That could become one of VivanteCare’s strongest competitive advantages.
